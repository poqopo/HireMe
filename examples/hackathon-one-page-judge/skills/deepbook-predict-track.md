# DeepBook Predict Track Skill

Use this skill when the target track is DeepBook Predict.

## Core Track Thesis

The project should build an innovative product, service, vault, bot, analytics tool, or developer tool around DeepBook Predict: a programmable, volatility-surface-priced prediction protocol on Sui.

The track is not looking for generic prediction-market branding. It wants projects that use Predict's actual market structure, testnet contract, oracle surface, or composability with the Sui DeFi stack.

## Problem The Track Is Solving

Prediction markets are fragmented and shallow. Many venues only support narrow binary events, settle slowly, cannot price strikes or ranges, and lack a real volatility surface. This prevents serious quant strategies, structured products, and on-chain risk transfer.

DeepBook Predict is about:
- pricing every strike and expiry against a live volatility surface
- short rolling expiries
- vault liquidity and auditable LP economics
- primitives that compose with Sui DeFi instead of staying inside one app

## What The Track Wants

Good project shapes include:
- user-facing prediction or trading frontends
- vaults, structured products, or tokenized shares built on Predict
- bots, keepers, or arbitrage services
- analytics dashboards, SDKs, indexers, or developer tooling

Especially strong directions:
- vault strategies across Predict positions, ranges, and PLP supply
- cross-venue arbitrage against Polymarket, Hyperliquid, or other markets
- gamified, mobile-first, Telegram, social, or alternate trading frontends
- live volatility surface viewers, PLP risk dashboards, PnL attribution, settlement leaderboards, or oracle health monitors
- composability with DeepBook spot, deepbook_margin, iron_bank, or tokenized PredictManager shares
- keeper services for settlement, redeem, oracle monitoring, or withdrawal-limiter watching

## Minimum Requirements

To be prize-qualified, the project should:
- integrate the DeepBook Predict contract on testnet
- work end to end if it is a product
- include proper simulation results if it is a vault strategy

## High-Scoring Evidence

- transaction, contract, or API proof that DeepBook Predict is integrated
- clear use of dUSDC, PredictManager, mint/redeem, PLP, or oracle/indexer surfaces when relevant
- a product or strategy that depends on Predict's volatility-surface model
- risk dashboards or simulations for vault and LP strategies
- clear path to mainnet-day-one usefulness

## Red Flags

- generic prediction-market UI with no Predict contract integration
- no testnet flow
- no simulation for a vault or strategy
- betting app that ignores strikes, expiries, vol surface, PLP, or composability
- analytics that do not use real Predict data
